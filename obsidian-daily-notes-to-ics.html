Markdown & Calendar

A tiny local-only web app for shuttling time-blocked notes and calendar events.

Convert Obsidian daily-note time blocks into calendar events — fully offline, right in your browser. No data leaves your device. Supports subfolders, #tags, @labels, and all major calendar formats (.ics, .ical, .icalendar, .ifb).

Free & open source. Found it useful? 🐙 Star it 🤩 on GitHub — it means a lot 😍
